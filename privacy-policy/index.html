Privacy & Data Transparency

Privacy Policy

Learn how Mimora protects your digital privacy. All interactive media tools operate 100% on-device — your files are never transmitted to our servers.

Core Guarantee: Your Files Never Leave Your Machine

When you merge or trim videos, edit PDFs, upscale photos, compress images, or format data on Mimora, the processing is performed in your browser's local sandbox using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript. We have no servers intercepting, uploading, or storing your files.

Zero File Storage

Because tools run locally in your browser memory, closing the tab permanently wipes all loaded file buffers from RAM.

No Accounts or Profiles

You do not need to register, provide an email address, or log in to use any utility on Mimora.

No Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal files or inputs under any circumstances.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mimora (“the site”, “we”, “us”) processes when you visit and use our website, how that data is handled, and what rights you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the platform.

2. Information Handled Automatically (Server Logs & CDN)

When you visit any page on Mimora, standard internet connection logs are automatically received by our hosting infrastructure and content delivery network (Cloudflare) to route traffic and prevent abuse:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser user-agent string (device, browser version, operating system)
  • Requested URL and HTTP referrer
  • Time and date stamp of requests
  • Approximate geographic region (country or city derived from IP)

These standard logs are retained temporarily for diagnostic and security purposes and are never linked to personal identities.

3. File and User Input Privacy

All interactive multimedia utilities on Mimora execute exclusively client-side:

  • Video & Audio Processing: Runs using WebAssembly-compiled FFmpeg directly inside your web browser. Videos are read from your disk into a sandboxed Virtual Worker File System and never uploaded.
  • PDF Tools: All merging, splitting, signing, and text annotation operations run via local JavaScript without transmission to any cloud server.
  • Text & Code Tools: Formatters, decoders, Base64 converters, and hash utilities operate instantly on your local CPU.

The only time you send data to our servers is if you choose to submit a message via our Contact Page.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Mimora uses minimal, standard cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Store user preferences such as Dark Mode / Light Mode toggles.
  • Analytics: Standard anonymous web analytics (such as Google Analytics) may be used to measure aggregate visitor numbers and popular pages to help us improve tool performance. You can disable analytics at any time via your browser's Do Not Track settings or ad-blocker extensions.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Privacy)

Because we do not maintain accounts or store your uploaded files, we possess virtually no personal data about you. If you have reached out through our contact form, you have the right to request review or deletion of your email communication at any time by contacting us.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to reflect new browser standards and features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated modification date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries regarding Mimora, please reach out via our Contact Page.
