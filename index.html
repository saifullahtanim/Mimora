Tools is a growing library of free online tools that run entirely in your browser — plus prank screens for fooling your friends and step-by-step tutorials for getting real work done. When a tool isn’t enough, the tutorials and blog cover the surrounding questions — server management, web development, and practical how-tos — written from real problems, not keyword lists. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tools?

Tools is a collection of free browser-based tools, prank screens, and step-by-step tech tutorials. Everything runs in your browser with no signup.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use with no accounts, trials, or paywalls.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. Open any tool and use it immediately — there is nothing to register for.

Is my data safe when I use a tool?

The tools are built to process files and text locally in your browser, so your data stays on your device unless a tool explicitly says otherwise.

Do the tools work on mobile?

Yes. Every page is responsive and works in mobile browsers; heavy file work is simply faster on a desktop.

Are the prank screens safe to run?

Completely. They are just web pages that imitate update screens, error screens, and lock screens. Nothing is installed and pressing ESC or closing the tab ends the prank instantly.

How do the tools work without uploading my files?

The processing code — the same kind of engines desktop software uses, compiled to WebAssembly — downloads to your browser and runs on your own device. Your file is opened and converted locally, which is why large files start instantly and why nothing ever reaches a server.

Who is behind Tools?

Tools is an independent site built and maintained by a small team of developers. You can read more on the About page, and the contact form goes straight to the people who build the tools.
